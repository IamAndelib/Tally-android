# Charges and extras — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or
> superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make fees, interest and tax part of the entry they came with: a **transfer charge** on transfers, loan
payments and lends, a **charge** on spending, **charges** (one total) on a borrow, and an **extra** on a lend (interest
agreed) or a lending's payback (a tip), through the approved tonal card; charges count as spending (a purchase's in its
own category, the rest in one grey Charges part on Home and in Summary), a lend's transfer charge and extra are owed
back, a borrow's charges are spending when paid back, extras are income when received. Money received carries none.
Saved data version 8. It is the last item before the 1.4.5 release (the release gate in
`docs/plans/2026-11-01-release-1.4.5.md`).

**Architecture:** Whatever moves money now is an entry linked both ways to its entry (`feeOf` on it, `feeId` on the
entry): a charge is an `expense`, a tip on a payback an `income`, a lend's transfer charge a `loan` draw entry of the
same lending. What only changes what is owed (a lend's extra, a borrow's charges) is a field on the draw, `onTop`.
`loanInfo()` (`js/loans.js`) works out what each draw owes, shares payments out (each draw in turn, due soonest, its
amount, then its transfer charge, then its amount on top) and which parts count as income or spending and when;
`loanParts()` gathers those parts once per render. One counting rule in `js/state.js` (`isSpend()` = every expense,
`spendCat()` = a category or `CHARGES`, plus `loanParts()`) is followed by every total: `spentOn()` (widget, morning
check), Home (`js/screens.js`, `js/ring.js`), History (`js/history.js`), Summary (`js/summary.js`) and search
(`js/search.js`). One shared form part, the card (`addOnCard()` in `js/sheets.js`, one or two rows, replacing
`feeField()`), with the keypad naming its field (`js/calculator.js`), serves every form (`js/sheets.js`,
`js/loans.js`); one helper (`setAddOn()` in `js/state.js`) makes, updates or removes a linked add-on so every form,
edit and the overpaid dialog keep its account, date and category with its entry.

**Tech Stack:** Classic scripts in one global scope (WebView 87+), CSS in `rem`, Playwright e2e suites in `tests/e2e`,
catalogues in `l10n/web/*.json` generated into `js/i18n/` by `tools/gen_i18n.py`.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/charges.md` (approved 2026-11-07; nothing open). Approved images: the design round's C-transfer,
C-rows, C-summary, C-forms and the tonal card B; round 2 in the scratchpad's `charges2/` (D9-home without its line and
with the amount under "Charges", D6-transfer A, D2-extras, D11-words); round 3 in `charges3/` (lend-form, borrow-form,
lend-after, lend-payback, lend-refine; its "Paid in all" boxes replaced as lend-refine shows).

## Global Constraints

- **Data safety:** the version 8 step in `migrate()` never changes an amount, account, date or balance; every loan
  recorded before version 8 gets exactly the same `loanInfo()`; both ship with their tests in Tasks 1 and 2, before
  any screen changes; a v7 save and a v7 backup load unchanged; a v8 backup restored by an older version keeps every
  balance; no later task changes the format without a test.
- **Nothing about a loan's amounts is stored on the loan:** owed totals, the share-out, and the parts counted as
  income or spending (and their days) are always worked out from the entries.
- **Tally never files an entry under a category by itself:** a charge counts in its purchase's category (the user's
  pick) or in the fixed Charges part, never in the built-in Fees category, whatever it was renamed to; extras are
  received, never a category's.
- **Totals agree:** for one period and currency, Home's figures, Summary's total, History's default heading, the
  widget and the morning check are the same numbers; every task that touches a total checks it.
- WebView 87: no `:has()`, `:is()`, `structuredClone`, `color-mix()`.
- Every font size in `rem`; boxes holding text use `min-height`; words wrap, never cut or shrink (`22-layout.js`).
- Every visible text through `T()` / `TH()`, keys in `l10n/web/en.json` and all eight other languages with the
  spec's words for "charge" and "extra" (each task translates its own new keys), then `python3 tools/gen_i18n.py`;
  never Prettier on `js/i18n/`.
- 48 dp targets; nothing by colour alone (a word and a sign are always beside the grey or the green); the grey
  `#7a8190` for the dot and slice only, never text; WCAG AA contrast in light and dark.
- The keypad and Android's keyboard are never on screen together.
- Don't reuse existing class names for new effects.
- Every visible change matches the approved images and texts.
- Commits end with only `Co-Authored-By: Claude <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
