# Charges and extras — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or
> superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make fees, interest and tax one thing, a **charge**, part of the entry it came with, offered on the transfer,
loan payment, lend, borrow and spending forms through the approved tonal card and counted as spending (a purchase's in
its own category, the rest in one grey Charges part on Home and in Summary); and money on top of a lending's payback an
**extra**, entered the same way and counted as received. Money received carries neither. Saved data version 8. It is
the last item before the 1.4.5 release (the release gate in `docs/plans/2026-11-01-release-1.4.5.md`).

**Architecture:** A charge or extra stays its own entry linked both ways to its entry (`feeOf` on it, `feeId` on the
entry), as transfer and payment fees are today; version 8 widens which entries may carry one: a charge (`expense`) on
a transfer, a loan entry or a purchase, an extra (`income`) only on a lending's payment. One counting rule in
`js/state.js` (`isSpend()` = every expense, `spendCat()` = the part it counts in: a category or `CHARGES`; an extra is
income) is followed by every total: `spentOn()` (widget, morning check), Home (`js/screens.js`, `js/ring.js`), History
(`js/history.js`), Summary (`js/summary.js`) and search (`js/search.js`). One shared form part, the card
(`addOnCard(kind)` in `js/sheets.js`, kind "charge" or "extra", replacing `feeField()`), with the keypad naming its
field (`js/calculator.js`), serves the transfer, payment, draw and spent forms (`js/sheets.js`, `js/loans.js`). One
helper makes, updates or removes an entry's charge or extra (`setAddOn()` in `js/state.js`), so every form, edit and
the overpaid dialog keep its account, date and category with its entry.

**Tech Stack:** Classic scripts in one global scope (WebView 87+), CSS in `rem`, Playwright e2e suites in `tests/e2e`,
catalogues in `l10n/web/*.json` generated into `js/i18n/` by `tools/gen_i18n.py`.

**Spec:** `docs/specs/charges.md` (approved 2026-11-07, every decision settled). Approved images: the design round's
C-transfer, C-rows, C-summary, C-forms, the tonal card B, and the second round's D9-home (without its leader line,
with the amount under "Charges"), D6-transfer (A), D2-extras and D11-words, in the scratchpad's `charges2/`.

## Global Constraints

- **Data safety:** the version 8 step in `migrate()` never changes an amount, account, date or balance; it ships with
  its tests in Task 1, before any screen changes; a v7 save and a v7 backup load unchanged; a v8 backup restored by an
  older version keeps every balance; no later task changes the format without a test.
- **Tally never files an entry under a category by itself:** a charge counts in its purchase's category (the user's
  pick) or in the fixed Charges part, never in the built-in Fees category, whatever it was renamed to; an extra is
  received, never a category's.
- **Totals agree:** for one period and currency, Home's figures, Summary's total, History's default heading, the
  widget and the morning check are the same numbers; every task that touches a total checks it.
- WebView 87: no `:has()`, `:is()`, `structuredClone`, `color-mix()`.
- Every font size in `rem`; boxes holding text use `min-height`; words wrap, never cut or shrink (`22-layout.js`).
- Every visible text through `T()` / `TH()`, keys in `l10n/web/en.json` and all eight other languages with the
  spec's words for "charge" and "extra" (each task translates its own new keys), then `python3 tools/gen_i18n.py`;
  never Prettier on `js/i18n/`.
- 48 dp targets; nothing by colour alone (a word and a sign are always beside the grey or the green); the grey
  `#7a8190` for the dot and slice only, never text; WCAG AA contrast in light and dark.
- The keypad and Android's keyboard are never on screen together.
- Don't reuse existing class names for new effects.
- Every visible change matches the approved images and texts.
- Commits end with only `Co-Authored-By: Claude <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

## Review Focus

1. Balances before and after the migration, and in an older version reading a v8 backup (every kind of charge, an
   extra, an older lending payment's paid fee).
2. A charge or extra always moves, deletes and undoes with its entry: edit (account, date, category), Delete (both
   answers), Undo, a payment's and a draw's edit, the loan sheet's delete, reopening a cleared loan, deleting a loan.
3. Totals agree across Home, Summary, History, search, the widget and the morning check, in two currencies; extras
   only in received.
4. The renamed Fees category ("Food") and a new notebook without Fees: no charge ever shows under a category.
5. The keypad on a card and on the amount: its bar's name and live result, the idle field, Back, a phone held
   sideways, large text, 360 px, German and Russian.
