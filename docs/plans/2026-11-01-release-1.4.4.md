# Tally 1.4.4: release plan

Patch release (maintainer, 2026-11-01): `tallyVersion=1.4.4`, `tallyVersionCode=10404`. 1.5.0 is the later big update.
Tick a box only when its proving check has passed, in the same pull request.

**How each item is built** (maintainer, 2026-11-03):
1. **Demo first.** Before any code changes, screenshots of the proposal: the real app at phone width, light and dark,
   with the change mocked on top by a throwaway script in the scratchpad (nothing in the repository). Options side by
   side where there is a choice.
2. **Discuss.** The maintainer picks or adjusts; the decision goes into `docs/decisions.md`, notes into `docs/specs/`.
3. **Build** what was agreed, with its tests (`npm run lint`, the suite for the area, then the full `npm test`).
4. **Try it:** push, wait for CI, send the Tally Dev APK; the maintainer installs it on a phone.

**Batches** (one demo round and one test APK each, in this order):
- A · items 1–2, the fixes · B · items 3–5, Year · C · items 6–7, accounts · D · item 8, search · then 11, polish.

## Fixes (maintainer's own observations)

- [ ] 1. **Thousands grouping while typing in the calculator** ("1,250 + 300") · S · bounded
      Maintainer: grouping separators in the expression as it is typed. Today the expression has no grouping
      (`calculator.js:177`). Use the phone's own separators (`NUMSEP`), never a fixed ","; keep caret and ⌫ right
      (`.caretmirror` hit-testing) and `evalAmt()` reading it.
      Demo: the keypad with "1,250 + 300" while typing, in English and German (1.250 + 300).
- [ ] 2. **Reminders for loans / lendings without a due day** · S–M · bounded
      Today none: `bridge.js:65` sends only loans with `nextDue`; `tests/e2e/20-loan-dues.js:194` asserts it (change
      that test deliberately). Maintainer: **monthly, on the day it was lent, both directions**, until paid back. A
      day missing from a month (lent on the 31st) falls on that month's last day; which draw's date counts when
      several are undated: decide in the design (oldest still out is the likely answer). New notification wording
      ("still owes you", not "due"), in every language; respects the existing "dues" switch and time.
      (Dropped 2026-11-01: auto-picking tomorrow as the due day.)
      To decide at the demo: the reminder's wording, e.g. "Sam still owes you $50 · lent 3 Oct" / "You still owe
      Maya $350 · borrowed 12 Sep"; whether the first one comes a month after the money moved (proposed) or sooner;
      whether "+1 day / +1 week" stay (snooze this month's reminder) or become "Set a due day". The page works out
      the next day and sends it with the dues (`bridge.js`), marked as having no due day; `ReminderReceiver` picks
      the wording. New Android texts in all nine languages.

## Features from issues

- [ ] 3. **Year as a period on Home and History** (#41 B) · S–M · feature
      Year tab beside Day / Range / Month (`period.js:141`); the balance bar shows the year's spent / received.
      To decide at the demo: the Year tab's picker (a grid of years, like the month grid) and what Home's ring and
      balance bar read for a year. History gets the same period.
- [ ] 4. **Year in the Summary** (#41 A) · S–M · feature
      Fourth Summary tab: 12 monthly bars, the year's categories, compared with the year before (`summary.js` modes).
      To decide at the demo: the bars as **years** (the last few, the same pattern as Days / Weeks / Months, where
      each bar is one unit) or as **the 12 months of one year**. Either way the year's categories below, compared
      with the year before, and "Open this year on Home".
- [ ] 5. **Choose what Home opens on** (#41 C) · S · bounded (needs 3)
      Setting "Open on: Today / This month / This year". New saved setting → `migrate()` sanitises it + test.
      To decide at the demo: where the setting lives (Settings → Region, beside the first day of the week, is
      proposed). A month starting on payday or "last 30 days" (asked in #41) stays out of 1.4.4.
- [ ] 6. **Account colour in History** (#37) · S · bounded
      Colour pill around the account name on each row; the name stays, so nothing is shown by colour alone.
      Demo: the pill and a coloured left edge side by side, light and dark, at a large text size; the pill's text
      must pass WCAG AA on every account colour.
- [ ] 7. **Reorder accounts** (#33, first part) · S · bounded
      Drag to reorder, reusing the category reorder (`gestures.js:334`).
      To decide at the demo: reorder in Assets (hold and drag, as moving a row to Archived works today), with Home's
      balance chips following that order; whether Home's chips can be dragged too.
- [ ] 8. **Global search** (#37 E, maintainer's design) · M · feature
      Search button to the left of the History button on Home, and in History. Searches notes, payees (and more:
      decide in spec). Placement confirmed from the maintainer's screenshots.
      To decide at the demo: what it searches (notes, payees, category and account names, amounts), how results
      look (History's rows, grouped by day, all time, newest first) and what a tap does (opens the entry).

Items 9 (password-protected backups) and 10 (app lock) moved to 1.5.0: see docs/roadmap.md.
