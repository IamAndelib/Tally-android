# Google Play store listing (copy and paste into Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing)

**App name** (max 30): Tally: Pocket Money Notebook

**Short description** (max 80):
One tap per expense. Every account, every currency. Private, offline, no ads.

**Full description** (max 4000):

Tally works like a pocket notebook for your money. Each morning your balances sit at the top of the page, you write
down spending through the day with one tap, and everything rolls over to tomorrow.

ONE TAP PER EXPENSE
Your spending categories sit around a donut of today's spending. Tap a category, type the amount, save. A built-in
calculator handles quick sums like 12 + 4.50.

EVERY ACCOUNT, EVERY CURRENCY
Bank accounts, mobile wallets like bKash, cash, credit cards and savings, each in its own currency (CAD, BDT, USD, EUR,
INR and more).

TRANSFERS THAT NEVER COUNT AS SPENDING
Move money between your own accounts, even between currencies, and add an ATM or cash-out fee when there is one.

FIX A BALANCE IN SECONDS
If an account doesn't match what you really have, type the real amount and Tally records the difference.

LOANS AND LENDINGS
Keep track of money you borrowed or lent: several amounts per person, due dates, partial payments, reminders and
write-offs. See your net worth on the Assets and Liabilities tabs.

LOOK BACK
History for any day, week, range or month, with each account's balance after every entry. Tap the donut for your last
7 days, 8 weeks or a month by category.

REMINDERS AND A WIDGET
An evening nudge if you haven't written anything today, loan due-date reminders, and a home-screen widget with today's
balance, today's spending and a quick add.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
No account, no ads, no tracking, no internet needed. Everything is stored on your phone. Back up to a file whenever
you like, restore it on a new phone, or export all entries as CSV.

Follows your phone's Material You colours, in light and dark.
