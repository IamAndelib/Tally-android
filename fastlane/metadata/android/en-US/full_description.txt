Tally works like a pocket notebook for your money. Each morning your balances sit at the top of the page, you write
down spending through the day with one tap, and everything rolls over to tomorrow.

ONE TAP PER EXPENSE
Your spending categories sit around a donut of today's spending. Tap a category, type the amount, save. A built-in
calculator handles quick sums like 12 + 4.50. Note who you paid or who paid you, with names you've used before
offered as you type.

EVERY ACCOUNT, EVERY CURRENCY
Bank accounts, mobile wallets, cash, credit cards and savings, each in its own currency: any of the world's
currencies.

MOVE MONEY BETWEEN YOUR ACCOUNTS
Move money between your own accounts, even between currencies, and add a charge, such as an ATM or cash-out fee, which
counts as spending.

FIX A BALANCE IN SECONDS
If an account doesn't match what you really have, type the real amount and Tally records the difference.

LOANS AND LENDINGS
Keep track of money you borrowed or lent: several amounts per person, due dates, partial payments (with any
charge, or an extra on top), reminders and write-offs. See your net worth on the Assets and Liabilities tabs.

LOOK BACK
History for any day, date range, month, year or all time, with each account's balance after every entry. Search it, filter by
account, kind, category or person, and archive entries you'd rather not see. Tap the donut for your spending by day,
week, month or year.

REMINDERS AND A WIDGET
An evening nudge if you haven't written anything today, loan due-date reminders, and a home-screen widget with today's
balance, today's spending and a quick add.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
No account, no ads, no tracking, no internet needed. Everything is stored on your phone. Back up to a file whenever
you like, restore it on a new phone, or export all entries as CSV.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
English, Español, Português (Brasil), Français, Deutsch, Русский, 简体中文, Bahasa Indonesia and Türkçe: Tally follows
your phone's language, and writes numbers, dates and money the way your phone does.

Follows your phone's Material You colours, in light and dark.
